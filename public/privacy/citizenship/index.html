Privacy Policy

Citizenship & Naturalisation Test Prep apps  ·  Effective 7 September 2026

These apps are built to work offline, with no account and no sign-in. Your study progress, scores, notes and settings never leave your phone. We do not run ads, we do not use analytics or tracking, and we never sell or share your data. The only time the app talks to the internet is to download study content, to verify a one-time Pro purchase, and — if you use the optional AI Tutor — to answer your question.

2 Data the app keeps on your device

All of the following is stored only on your device, in the app’s private storage. It is never uploaded to us, and we cannot see it:

  • Study progress — questions answered, correct/incorrect history, spaced-repetition state, mastery per topic
  • Mock test results — scores and history used for the readiness / pass predictor
  • Streaks, XP and daily goals
  • Bookmarks, mistakes journal, and handbook highlights & notes
  • Settings — chosen test date, question language, reminder preferences, light/dark theme

You can erase all of it at any time with Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.

7 Data retention & deletion

On your device: kept until you use Settings → Reset progress, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app. We hold no copy.

Purchases: RevenueCat and Google Play retain purchase records so your Pro unlock can be restored. To request deletion of the purchase record linked to your anonymous app ID, email us and we will action it within 30 days.

AI Tutor: we keep no conversation history on our servers.

8 Children’s privacy

These apps are study tools for adults preparing for a citizenship or naturalisation test. They are not directed at children, and because the apps collect no personal information, none is collected from children.

10 Security

Every network request the app makes uses HTTPS/TLS encryption. Data stored on your device sits in the app’s private, OS-sandboxed storage. Payment card details are never seen or stored by us — all payments are handled by Google Play.
