ArrowGo

Privacy Policy

Swipe. Paint. Solve. — an offline-first maze-fill puzzle game with no ads, no analytics, and no third-party trackers.

Last updated 5 September 2026
The short version: ArrowGo works completely offline. You do not need an account, and nothing you do in the game leaves your device unless you deliberately turn on cloud backup. There are no adverts, no analytics, and no third-party trackers.

What is stored on your device

Your progress is saved in a database on your phone: which levels you have completed, your stars, best move counts and times, coins, hints, daily puzzle results, achievements and the cosmetics you own.

Your settings are stored the same way: sound, music, haptics, appearance, and whether you have seen the tour.

Deleting the app removes all of it.

What leaves your device, and only if you ask

Cloud backup is off by default. If you sign in and turn it on, the progress and settings listed above are copied to our Supabase project so you can restore them on another device.

That copy is tied to your account and readable only by you. It is enforced by database rules on the server, not just by the app.

To sign in we store your email address and an authentication token. We never see or store your password.

You can sign out at any time, which stops any further syncing. Ask us to delete your account and the stored copy goes with it.

What we never collect

Your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Advertising identifiers or any cross-app tracking.

Analytics about how you play.

Children

ArrowGo is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone by default. If you choose to enable cloud backup, an email address is required, so that feature is intended for players aged 13 and over.

Purchases

Premium is handled by the App Store or Google Play. Payment details go to them and never reach us; the app only ever learns whether a purchase succeeded.

Your choices

Reset progress, in Settings, clears everything stored on this device.

Sign out, in Settings, stops cloud backup.

Uninstalling the app removes all local data.

Contact

Questions, or a request to delete your stored copy: ofbuilder.apps@gmail.com.
