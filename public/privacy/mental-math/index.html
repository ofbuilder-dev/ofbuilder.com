Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026 App version: 3.0 App package: com.thenoob.mental_math_master

Mental Math Master works offline. There is no account to create, and your progress is stored on your phone, not on our servers. This policy explains the little data the app does handle and the one service it talks to when you buy something.

  • No account or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics or tracking
  • Progress stays on your phone
  • We never sell your data

01 Data stored on your phone

To save your game, the app keeps the following in its private storage on your device:

  • Your progress: levels, stars, scores, personal records, streaks and trophies
  • Your coin and gem balance, and a history of how they were earned and spent
  • Items you own, such as themes, hints and VIP status
  • Your settings, including the level and daily goal you chose and your reminder time
  • A random install ID and a security key, used only to protect your coin balance from tampering

None of this is sent to us. We have no server that receives your game data, and we cannot see it.

02 Purchases

Buying coins, gems, VIP or other items is handled by Google Play. Google processes your payment; we never see your card or payment details.

The app uses RevenueCat to confirm purchases with Google Play and to check whether your VIP subscription is active. When you open the Shop, make a purchase or tap Restore purchases, RevenueCat receives:

  • A random, anonymous customer ID created by RevenueCat (not your name or email)
  • The purchase receipt from Google Play: what was bought, when, and the price and currency
  • Basic technical details such as your device's OS version, app version, store country and IP address

We use this only to give you what you paid for and to restore purchases on a new phone. Coins and gems are added to the balance stored on your phone.

03 Backups

Android backup

If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's progress and settings to your own Google account, so they come back when you install the app on a new phone. This is controlled by you and by Google, not by us. The security key used to protect your coin balance is never included.

Backup file

You can also export your progress as a .mmm file from Settings > Backup & restore and save or share it wherever you choose. The file stays wherever you put it, and we never receive it. Importing a file only reads the file you pick.

04 Notifications, sharing and feedback

Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your phone. No push service or server is involved.

Home-screen widget

The widget reads your streak and today's puzzle progress from the app on your phone.

Sharing results

When you tap Share, the app creates a picture of your result and opens your phone's share menu. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app to send it to.

Feedback and ratings

Send feedback opens your own email app with a message to us that includes your star rating, the topics you picked, your message and the app version. We receive it only if you send it, along with your email address, and use it only to reply and improve the app. Rating the app uses Google Play's in-app review, which is handled by Google.

05 Permissions

  • Internet: only for purchases through Google Play and RevenueCat. Every game works offline.
  • Notifications: only for the optional daily reminder. You can say no or turn it off at any time.
  • Run at startup: lets your daily reminder stay scheduled after the phone restarts.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

06 Third-party services

  • Google Play policies.google.com/privacy

    Processes payments and subscriptions, in-app reviews and, if you have it on, Android backup.

  • RevenueCat revenuecat.com/privacy

    Confirms purchases and subscription status on our behalf.

Each provider's own privacy policy covers how they handle data on their side. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

07 Children's privacy

Mental Math Master is made for teens and adults and is not directed at children under 13. The app does not ask for personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

08 Data security

Your game data lives in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Your coin and gem history is signed with a key kept in Android's secure storage, so it can't be quietly changed. Purchase checks with RevenueCat and Google Play are encrypted in transit. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we keep the data we handle to a minimum.

09 Deleting your data

  • Delete your progress at any time in Settings > Reset progress.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone.
  • Delete Android backups from your Google account settings, and any .mmm files wherever you saved them.
  • To delete feedback emails you sent us, or to ask about purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us below.
Google Play keeps its own record of your purchases and subscriptions, which you can view and manage in your Google account.

10 Changes to this policy

If we change what the app does with data, we will update this page and the effective date at the top.
