How to request account deletion

You can ask us to delete your IlmAI account and the data linked to it at any time. Follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to ofbuilder.apps@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in to IlmAI.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete My Account — IlmAI".
  3. We will verify the request and permanently delete your account and data within 30 days. We will email you when it is done.
Email deletion request
Email: ofbuilder.apps@gmail.com
Subject: Delete My Account — IlmAI

Deleting your account does not cancel an active Pro subscription. Please cancel it first in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, so you are not charged again.

What data is deleted

DataWhat happens
Account info (email address, name, user ID) Deleted
Profile settings (language, madhab, Pro status) Deleted
Chat history (questions and AI answers) Deleted
Saved answers Deleted
Purchase records held by Google Play and RevenueCat Kept as required by Google Play and RevenueCat for billing, tax and legal purposes, under their own retention policies
Data stored on your phone (cached chats, settings, downloaded Quran files) Removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data

We do not keep any other copy of your account data after deletion is complete.

Delete only some data

To delete individual conversations or saved answers without deleting your account, delete them inside the app. You can also email us at ofbuilder.apps@gmail.com and tell us which data to remove.
